Meaning

Order
What a customer buys in one checkout.
Finance calls it a "sale" and the warehouse calls it a "shipment". Same thing, three names.
SKU
One specific product variant the warehouse stocks.
One T-shirt in three sizes is three SKUs, not one product.
Back-order
An order accepted for something that's out of stock.
The customer has paid, but nothing ships until stock arrives.
