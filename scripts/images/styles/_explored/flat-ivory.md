Create a polished editorial hero illustration for a technical blog post. The attached images are earlier heroes from the same blog: match their visual style closely (palette, line-art icon style, soft tinted circles and panels, typography weight, amount of whitespace). Do not copy their content or layout.

Style:
- Warm technical editorial illustration: calm, credible, precise, spacious, human-readable.
- Warm ivory canvas (#FCFAF4) with cream panels (#F7F1E7, #FFFDF8). Keep roughly 70% of the image light and empty.
- Deep navy (#08184D) for the main title, charcoal (#33363E) for line art and labels.
- Restrained accents with meaning: orange (#F37A19) for the key phrase or emphasis, muted blue (#527DB7) for information and the second title line, soft green (#7FA85B) for outcomes and validation. Use pale tints of these for fills. At most three accent hues.
- Thin, rounded, single-weight line-art icons in charcoal with small accent-colored details. Soft tinted circles behind icon groups are fine.
- Thin muted-blue arrows and dotted connectors between stages.
- Crisp modern sans-serif (Inter-like), bold title, left-aligned or centered as the composition needs. Never stretch, squash, outline or add effects to text.

Constraints:
- One clear focal idea, readable as a small thumbnail.
- Only the text listed in the brief below, spelled exactly as given. No extra words, no lorem ipsum, no fake UI text.
- Keep labels short and large.
- Avoid: neon or purple gradients, dark backgrounds, glowing orbs, glassmorphism, robots, brains, circuit patterns, photorealism, 3D renders, dense tables, heavy black borders, hard shadows.
