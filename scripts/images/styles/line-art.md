Create a refined, minimal hero illustration for a technical blog post in a LINE-ART style, like an elegant engineering blueprint or the diagrams in a premium developer-docs site: precise, quiet, intelligent. Not flashy.

Look and feel:
- A clean off-white canvas (#FCFAFD) with a barely visible fine dot grid.
- Everything is drawn with a single consistent thin stroke in deep purple (#320063), rounded line caps. Flat fills only, in pale lilac (#EEE7F8) and white. No 3D, no gradients, no shadows beyond a hairline offset.
- ONE focal element is filled in solid magenta (#EA2775), with a small coral (#EA8170) highlight. Everything else stays purple line and pale fill.
- No glow, no particles, no light beams, no neon.
- Typography: large, bold, rounded geometric sans like Nunito. The title is deep purple, left-aligned. The subtitle is lighter weight, in magenta. Labels are small, in deep purple, set in clean sans.
- Lots of negative space and precise alignment, as if drawn by a senior information designer.

Constraints:
- Only the text listed in the brief, spelled exactly. No other words, no fake UI text, no logos.
- No brains, no robots, no faces, no holograms, no circuit boards.
